# The Forgotten King and the Plague Bell

A forgotten king claims a throne that is not his, while a plague bell rings out sickness and fear across the land. Linked with the Wolf, the Black Fountain, and automated systems of control, the darkness is overthrown and silenced by the true King, Jesus Christ.
