# Album 81 – Story and Story Song Method with Prayer (Spoken) | SCP-Themed Intercession | KJC-DNN / Remnant Christian Worship

Spoken intercessory prayers from KJC-DNN / Remnant Christian Worship confronting numbered anomalies (SCP ranges 1601–2000 and related national designations) through the authority of Jesus Christ. Contains seven spoken-word tracks of spiritual warfare, binding, overthrow, and restoration. All glory to Jesus Christ alone.

Numbered Shadows Bound is Album 81 in the KJC-DNN / Remnant Christian Worship series.

This album uses a multi-layered structure for each track:

    Story
    Story Song (Spoken Prayer)
    Song of Parables (Jesus)
    Psalms (David)
    Proverbs (Solomon)
    Haiku Song
    Riddle Song

The seven tracks confront spiritual forces associated with numbered anomalies and false systems (sigils, chaos, occult patterns, false restoration, shadow choirs, forgotten thrones, memory theft, and related darkness). Every piece declares the authority of Jesus Christ to bind, overthrow, silence, and restore.
